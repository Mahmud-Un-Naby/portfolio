PROJECT IDENTITY

SOLO PROJECT · WEB & ANDROID

FailureOJ

A competitive problem-solving platform for math and science. Students submit answers and notebook images, practice in English or Bangla, and compete in contests with standings and ratings.

React & TypeScriptNode.jsPython & SymPyPostgreSQLRedisCapacitor
Inside the project
  • Math-aware judging. A Python/FastAPI service uses SymPy to compare numeric expressions, including equivalent fractions and roots, with tolerance-based checking.
  • Contest workflows. Fixed or dynamic scoring, standings, ratings, and participant challenges reviewed by contest judges.
  • Learning and community. Bilingual problems, editorials, tutorials, problem collections, and groups, with an Android build using Capacitor.

Engineering focus: the answer judge runs separately from the Express API. A Redis-backed queue and retry logic handle deferred judging, while Socket.IO delivers verdict updates. PostgreSQL and Prisma store the platform’s data.
